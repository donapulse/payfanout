`capturePayment` without an amount now captures only the uncaptured remainder of a PayPal authorization; earlier versions asked PayPal for the full authorized amount again, which PayPal's overage limit could accept after a partial capture, so review manual-capture payments captured that way. A capture that reaches the remainder, with or without an amount, now closes the authorization and later captures against it are refused; capturing the rest of a fully captured payment returns the payment without sending a capture to PayPal, capturing the rest of a voided or denied authorization rejects with `invalid_request`, and capturing and cancelling accept the capture id an earlier capture returned. `amountRefunded` leaves out failed and cancelled refunds, a completion repeated under a new idempotency key returns the existing capture or authorization instead of failing, refund reason codes are no longer sent to the payer as `note_to_payer`, Venmo-funded orders report `paymentMethodDetails.wallet: "venmo"`, and a payment read from a capture whose order has aged out leaves `paymentMethodDetails` out instead of reporting the PayPal wallet.
