Request native 3-D Secure 2 on card payments whose `clientToken` from `@payfanout/adapter-adyen` carries the browser data for it, sending `channel: "Web"`, `origin`, `browserInfo` and `nativeThreeDS: "preferred"`, plus the billing address and risk data when present. Only those fields are read from the browser, and a `paymentMethod` that is not a card or carries unencrypted card fields is refused with `invalid_request`; the bare `paymentMethod` token of earlier client adapters still completes as before. A 3-D Secure action answered without a `pspReference` now reads `requires_action` with an empty `pspPaymentId` instead of failing with `processing_error`. A `/payments/details` answer whose `merchantReference` or `amount` differs from the session is refused with `invalid_request`, and one that omits either reads `processing` until the `AUTHORISATION` webhook settles the payment. Session creation now refuses a `returnUrl` that is not absolute with a scheme, is longer than 1024 characters or has `//` after the domain, and such a `defaultReturnUrl` is refused when the adapter is constructed. `shopperEmail` falls back to `billingDetails.email` when there is no `receiptEmail`, and an email that is not an address of at most 256 characters is refused at session creation. `shopperIP` is not sent: the adapter has no shopper IP address to send.
