`refundPayment` and `retrievePayment` now find the settlement of a manually captured Paysafe payment, and of a payment settled more than 30 days ago. A capture of the whole authorization (no amount, or the authorized amount) now settles under a reference derived from the payment, `payfanout-capture-<pspPaymentId>`, instead of the capture's idempotency key, so that reference, not your key, is the settlement's `merchantRefNum` in the Paysafe portal. After a full capture that moved no money (failed, cancelled or expired), the next one settles under `-a2`, up to `-a10`, whatever its key; a full capture that finds the payment already captured in full answers with that settlement and sends nothing, a capture of the authorized amount after the rest was captured is refused as already captured in full, and a capture with no amount when nothing is left is refused as nothing left to capture instead of sending an empty settlement. A partial capture still settles under its idempotency key, which may no longer start with `payfanout-capture-`; the adapter cannot find that settlement from the payment, so refund it in the Paysafe portal, using the settlement id every capture's answer now carries on `raw.captureSettlement`. Captures made by earlier releases settled under their idempotency keys too, so refund those in the portal as well. Settlement lookups start the day before the payment instead of covering Paysafe's default 30 days, and ask for 50 records; they are sent again without the range only when Paysafe refuses it, and any other lookup failure now fails the read, refund, cancel or capture (retryable for an outage or a rate limit) instead of reading as no settlement. A manually captured payment no longer looks its completion key up, and a declined, cancelled or expired payment no longer reports, or refunds, the settlement of another payment under the same reference. The refusal of a refund with nothing to refund now says why, from the payment's state.
