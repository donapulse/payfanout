Send ISK and MGA amounts in Stripe's units and convert the amounts Stripe reports in them back: ISK amounts are now multiplied by 100 (Stripe represents ISK as a two-decimal value, so ISK 1,000 was charged as ISK 10) and MGA amounts divided by 100 (a zero-decimal currency at Stripe, so MGA 10.00 was charged as MGA 1,000). MGA amounts that are not whole ariary, and every UGX amount, which Stripe's currency documentation gives two different units, are refused with `invalid_request` before the request that would carry them, marked `outcomeUnknown` on a saved-method charge or subscription create, which an earlier release may already have sent under the same key; reads of UGX payments, refunds and subscriptions reject with `unsupported_operation`, list pages holding one fail whole, and UGX webhook events carry no `amount`. Captures and refunds that state an amount, and session updates that carry only one of `amount` and `currency`, now read the PaymentIntent first, one more request each.

Before upgrading, route UGX to another provider, and move `SubscriptionManager` subscriptions that renew on Stripe in UGX, or in MGA amounts that are not whole ariary, whose renewals would now fail with `invalid_request`. ISK and MGA payments made before the upgrade now read back as what Stripe charged (an ISK 1,000 payment from then reads as ISK 10): reconcile them in the Stripe Dashboard, along with the Stripe Billing subscriptions created in them, which keep billing the price they were created with. A session created before the upgrade still charges the amount it was created with: cancel open ISK and MGA sessions, or re-send their amount with `updatePaymentSession`, right after upgrading.
