Send ISK and MGA amounts in Stripe's units and convert the amounts Stripe reports in them back: ISK is multiplied by 100, as Stripe represents it with two decimals (ISK 1,000 was charged as ISK 10), and MGA divided by 100, a zero-decimal currency at Stripe (MGA 10.00 was charged as MGA 1,000). Captures and refunds that state an amount, and session updates carrying only one of `amount` and `currency`, now read the PaymentIntent first, one more request each.

Breaking: UGX, which Stripe's currency documentation gives two different units, and MGA amounts that are not whole ariary are now refused with `invalid_request` before the request that would carry them. Where an earlier release may already have sent the same request under the same key (saved-method charges, subscription creates, captures and refunds unless the payment shows nothing captured or refunded yet, and amount updates of a UGX payment), the refusal is marked `outcomeUnknown`: check the Stripe Dashboard under that key before sending another. Reads of UGX payments, refunds and subscriptions reject with `unsupported_operation`, list pages holding one fail whole, and UGX webhook events carry no `amount`. ISK and MGA payments and subscriptions made before the upgrade read back 100 times differently, as what Stripe actually holds (an ISK 1,000 payment from then reads as ISK 10). `SubscriptionManager` renewals on Stripe in UGX, or in MGA amounts that are not whole ariary, freeze: each is pinned as a charge without a definitive answer, replayed, then held until `resolvePendingRenewal` settles it.

Before upgrading, route UGX to another provider and move those subscriptions. After upgrading, reconcile ISK and MGA payments made before the upgrade in the Stripe Dashboard, along with the Stripe Billing subscriptions created in them, which keep billing the price they were created with, and cancel open ISK and MGA sessions or re-send their amount with `updatePaymentSession`.
