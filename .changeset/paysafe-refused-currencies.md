Refuse the currencies Paysafe may price with another exponent than PayFanout instead of sending their amounts unchanged (CLP 10,000 was charged as CLP 100.00): CLP and BYR, which Paysafe's currency table prices differently, and BIF, CLF, DJF, GNF, IQD, ISK, KMF, UGX, UYI, UYW, VUV, XAF, XOF and XPF, which the table does not list and which are not priced in hundredths; route them to another provider. Sessions, saved-method charges and native subscriptions in them reject with a non-retryable `invalid_request` before any request, and captures, voids and refunds of a payment Paysafe holds in one read the payment, then reject before any settlement, void or refund request, with `invalid_request` when they state an amount and `unsupported_operation` otherwise. Reads of such payments and subscriptions, and of refunds Paysafe reports in one, reject with `unsupported_operation`, webhook events Paysafe reports in one carry no `amount`, and `cancelNativeSubscription` now reads the subscription first, one more request on every cancel, so a failed read fails the cancel before anything is sent. Before upgrading, move `SubscriptionManager` subscriptions that renew on Paysafe in these currencies to another provider, as their renewals would now fail with `invalid_request`, a definitive failure, and run dunning to cancellation. Native Paysafe subscriptions created in them keep billing at Paysafe's exponent (a CLP 10,000 plan bills CLP 100.00 every cycle) and the adapter can no longer read, list or cancel them: cancel them in the Paysafe portal and re-create them elsewhere.
