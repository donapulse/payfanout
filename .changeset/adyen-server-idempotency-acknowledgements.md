Before deploying, stop retrying any `completePayment` first sent by version 0.1.0 and wait for its `AUTHORISATION` webhook instead: the `idempotency-key` of `/payments` and `/payments/details` changes, so such a retry reaches Adyen as a new request and could charge the shopper twice, since Adyen captures payments immediately after authorisation by default. Captures, cancels and refunds keep theirs, so retrying one across the upgrade is still deduplicated.

The `/payments` and `/payments/details` key now also covers the merchant account, and on `/payments/details` the submitted `details` and `paymentData`: Adyen checks keys across the whole company account, so two merchant accounts sharing a caller key no longer receive each other's stored answers, and each step of a multi-step 3-D Secure flow reaches Adyen while a repeated step is still deduplicated. Captures, cancels and refunds now reject an acknowledgement without its own `pspReference` with a retryable `processing_error` (captures and cancels used to resolve on one, and refunds rejected it as a non-retryable `processing_error`), and a capture or refund acknowledgement echoing another amount or currency, which is Adyen's stored answer to an earlier request under the same key, with `invalid_request`. A 2xx response that is not a JSON object is a retryable `psp_unavailable`, also on `completePayment`.

Errors Adyen sends with `transient-error: true` are retried at any status, `errorCode` 705 maps to `rate_limited`, HTTP 408 to a retryable `psp_unavailable`, and HTTP 501 and 5xx errors typed `validation`, `configuration` or `security` to a non-retryable `invalid_request` instead of a retried `psp_unavailable`. Refusal reason 31 (Issuer Suspected Fraud) maps to `fraud_suspected`, and `refundPayment` sends the refund `reason` as Adyen's `merchantRefundReason`.
