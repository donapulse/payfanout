Stores must persist the new `SubscriptionRecord.renewalAttempt` field verbatim, like `pendingRenewal`: it is money-safety state. A renewal charge that ends without a definitive answer (an unreachable or rate-limiting provider, an unknown error, an error marked `outcomeUnknown`, or a first `processing_error`) is now replayed under the same idempotency key and request, on the new `replayDelaysMinutes` schedule and only within the new `replayWindowHours`; once no replay can safely follow it is frozen until `resolvePendingRenewal` settles it with its `idempotencyKey`, which every renewal charge carries in its metadata as `payfanout_renewal_key` (`parseRenewalIdempotencyKey` reads it back). Attempt numbers are never reused, a decline of a replaced card no longer counts against the new one, `resumeSubscription` refuses while such a charge is unsettled, `retryDelaysHours` now rejects non-finite values, and a charge failing while its record was paused or canceled now records `lastError` and emits `subscription.charge_failed`. `PaymentService` and `PaymentRouter` keep `outcomeUnknown` on the errors they rebuild.
