Stores must persist the new `SubscriptionRecord.renewalAttempt` field verbatim, like `pendingRenewal`, since it is money-safety state, and a `listDue` implementation should now leave out records waiting for `resolvePendingRenewal` (a `pendingRenewal`, or a frozen `renewalAttempt.replay`), as `InMemorySubscriptionStore` does. A renewal charge that ends without a definitive answer (an unreachable or rate-limiting provider, an unknown error, an error marked `outcomeUnknown`, or a first `processing_error`) is now replayed under the same idempotency key and request on the new `replayDelaysMinutes` schedule, within the new `replayWindowHours`, and otherwise frozen until `resolvePendingRenewal` settles it with the key every renewal charge carries as `payfanout_renewal_key` metadata (`parseRenewalIdempotencyKey` reads it back). A replaced card's decline no longer counts against the new one, `resumeSubscription` refuses while such a charge is unsettled, and `retryDelaysHours` rejects non-finite values.
