Let a customer pay with another card after a failed completion under the same idempotency key: Worldline answers a repeated key with its first outcome for at least 24 hours, so `completePayment` now makes a new attempt under a key derived from yours once every earlier attempt under it has failed, and otherwise returns the earlier attempt's payment, even one first answered with a decline that has since gone through. A decline whose answer may replay an earlier completion's or whose payment Worldline had not finished, a 2xx answer naming no payment, a payment Worldline could not read back, and a completion refused because Worldline could forget the key's first attempt before the session expires (`raw.reason: "first_attempt_may_expire"`) or because the key's earlier payment was for another amount or currency (`raw.reason: "another_sessions_payment"`) now carry `outcomeUnknown`, so retry them only under the same key. A key carries at most 20 attempts, after which completion is refused with a non-retryable `invalid_request` (`raw.reason: "attempt_limit"`).
