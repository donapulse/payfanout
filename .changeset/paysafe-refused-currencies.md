Refuse the currencies Paysafe may price with another exponent than PayFanout instead of sending their amounts unchanged: CLP and BYR, which Paysafe's currency table prices differently (per that table, CLP 10,000 was charged as CLP 100.00), and BIF, CLF, DJF, GNF, IQD, ISK, KMF, UGX, UYI, UYW, VUV, XAF, XOF and XPF, which the table does not list and which are not priced in hundredths. Sessions, saved-method charges and native subscriptions in them reject with a non-retryable `invalid_request` before any request; captures, voids and refunds of a payment Paysafe holds in one read the payment, then reject before any other request, with `invalid_request` when they state an amount and `unsupported_operation` otherwise; reads of such payments and subscriptions, and of refunds Paysafe reports in one, reject with `unsupported_operation`; and webhook events Paysafe reports in one carry no `amount`. `cancelNativeSubscription` now reads the subscription before cancelling it: one more request on every cancel, and a failed read fails the cancel before anything is sent.

Before upgrading, route these currencies to another provider and move `SubscriptionManager` subscriptions that renew on Paysafe in them, whose renewals would fail with `invalid_request`, a definitive failure that runs dunning to cancellation. Native Paysafe subscriptions created in them keep billing at Paysafe's exponent (a CLP 10,000 plan bills CLP 100.00 every cycle) and can no longer be read, listed or cancelled through the adapter: cancel them in the Paysafe portal and re-create them elsewhere. A session created in one before the upgrade fails to complete with `invalid_request` marked `outcomeUnknown`, as the earlier release may already have completed it: look for its payment in the Paysafe portal before charging the customer elsewhere.
